    Teach the agent your market

    1. Download M1 history

      Pulls days of 1-minute candles for from MT5 into data/.

    2. Add years of extra history

      Free XAUUSD 1-minute candles back to 2009 (HistData). 5 years is about 1.8 million candles, roughly 25× what MT5 keeps. Training uses it automatically; your MT5 candles win where they overlap. Each year is ~24 MB to download; training uses about 0.5 GB of RAM per year.

    3. Train on the RTX 4060

      XGBoost learns from the history, then is tested on the most recent quarter it never saw. Takes about 1–3 minutes.

    4. Paper trade it

      Training suggests a confidence threshold (the most profitable one with 100+ test trades). It appears on the Agent tab. Start in Paper mode.

      Tradingwhat the bot trades and how it behaves
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          The bot only opens new trades in these hours; open trades are managed all the time. Applies from the next Start. 0 to 24 (or the same hour twice) means all day; a start later than the end runs overnight, e.g. 22 to 6.
          
          Entries still use the Confidence threshold. The Bot trade card also shows where the model's best ~10% of readings start, to help you pick one.
          
          
          Blocks the hours, sides and setups that kept losing. See "What the bot has learned" on the Agent tab.
        
      
        
      Bot money rulesstake, stop and target for every bot trade
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          Closing early only costs what it actually lost; a stop-loss hit costs the penalty × the loss.
          
      Score: 1 point = $1. Each trade earns or loses its dollar result (a +$100 trade = +100 points). A stop-loss hit costs the penalty × its loss (−$20 × 1.5 = −30). A trade the bot closes early only costs what it actually lost (−$20 = −20).
      
          
      Stake is the margin the trade uses. If the stake is below the symbol's minimum lot, the minimum lot is used. Take profit eases from the small-stake % to the large-stake % as stakes grow. Retrain after changing the stop or take-profit rules so the model learns the new exits.
      
        
      
        
      News pauseno new bot trades around big news
          
          Gold jumps on US news (CPI, jobs, Fed decisions) and spreads widen. Trades already open are left alone; only new entries wait.
