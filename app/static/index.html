    Quiz school
    
          
    Real gold charts where one of 18 pro setups worked, where one failed (a trap: stay out), or where pros would stay out. The agent answers what do you do? and points are its only reward: right +10, wrong way −10, traded when it should stay out −5, missed a good trade −3. A question is finished once it's right 5 times in a row.

      Bot money rules
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      Score: 1 point = $1. Each trade earns or loses its dollar result (a +$100 trade = +100 points). A stop-loss hit costs the penalty × its loss (−$20 × 1.5 = −30). A trade the bot closes early only costs what it actually lost (−$20 = −20).
      
        
      Stake is the margin the trade uses. If the stake is below the symbol's minimum lot, the minimum lot is used. Take profit eases from the small-stake % to the large-stake % as stakes grow. Retrain after changing the stop or take-profit rules so the model learns the new exits.
