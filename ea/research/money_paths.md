# Automated ways to make money from ~$400 - research (2026-10-01)

Target asked for: $100/week from $400 = 25%/week, ~1,300%/year. No safe, regulated strategy does that; the
list below is what exists, with the evidence.

## 1. Solana trenching bot (AI + tweet monitor) - already in this app (`sol/`, Chat B)
- Paper by default; tweet radar, rug rules, model crew + debate, Jupiter swaps for live.
- Evidence (CoinGecko + Dune, Pump.fun/PumpSwap wallets): Apr 2024 - late 2025 most traders lost every month
  (30-45% profitable). Apr 2026: 73% profitable, but 65% of wallets made $1-$500 for the whole month and only
  5.4% made over $1,000. $100/week = top ~5-8%.
  https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback
- Galaxy Research: day-to-day memecoin trading is negative-EV for most; the money goes to the infrastructure
  (Axiom > $200M revenue). https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/memecoins-pump-fun-solana-kols
- To know if ours works: weeks of paper trading with fees, priority fees and slippage in the fills, then a small
  live stake. No paper results exist yet (sol.db lives on the user's PC).

## 2. Kalshi (CFTC-regulated prediction market, open API, bots allowed)
- Strategies people automate: market making, cross-venue arbitrage, data-driven markets (weather, economic data).
- Edge is real for some but competitive and unproven for us; $400 caps size. Can be built and paper-tested.

## 3. Futures prop firms (trade the firm's money, risk only the fee)
- Topstep allows API bots (TopstepX). Combine ~$99 for a $100k account: $6,000 target, $3,000 max loss.
- Topstep 2025: 16.8% of Combines passed; 33.3% of funded traders got a payout. Industry: ~7% of all buyers
  ever get paid. Needs an intraday edge; our only proven edge (daily gold trend) is too slow for a Combine.
- Prop firms are not regulated brokers.

## 4. Safe and regulated, but slow
- Daily gold trend bot on 1OZ futures: real edge (22 years), needs ~$2,000-4,000.
- Unlevered gold-fund trend rule: ~6%/yr. Treasury bills / high-yield savings: ~4%/yr ($400 -> ~$0.30/week).
- $100/week at 5%/yr needs ~$100,000.
