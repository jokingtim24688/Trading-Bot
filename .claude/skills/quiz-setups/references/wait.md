# No setup (stay out)

**Right answer: STAY OUT.**

## What the question shows
A quiet spot between 10:00 and 20:00 server time:
- Price is in the middle of the day's range (35-65% of it).
- There's no sweep and no opening-range break.
- It's 0.5-2.5 ATR from the session average, more than 1 ATR from a $50 round number, and more than 2 ATR from
  yesterday's high and low.

It's also checked forward: neither a buy nor a sell with a 1.5 ATR stop and a 2R target would have been a clean
trade.

## Why pros stay out
There is no level, no trapped traders and no imbalance: no edge. Trading here pays the spread for a coin flip.
Pros wait for a setup to come to them.

## Points
- Trading when it should stay out costs 5 points.
- Staying out is right: +10 points.

## If the agent keeps trading these
It has learned "something is always happening". Stay-out questions make up 20% of every quiz (at most 40% when
other groups run out), so it keeps getting corrected. If it's stuck here, the charts may look like one of the setups;
the weak-spot report shows which.
