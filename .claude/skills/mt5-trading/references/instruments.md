# Instrument Notes (M1 perspective)

## Gold, XAUUSD
- Quoted in USD per troy oz. Symbol variants: `XAUUSD`, `XAUUSD.m`, `XAUUSD.raw`, `GOLD`, `GOLD#`.
- Contract: usually 1 lot = 100 oz → $1 move = $100/lot. Some brokers use 1 lot = 1 oz or 10 oz, so check.
- Digits: 2 (0.01) or 3. Typical raw spread 8–25 points (0.08–0.25) in the overlap; 50+ at rollover.
- M1 ATR(14): often 0.8–2.5 USD in the active sessions; far more around US data.
- Drivers: USD (DXY, inverse), US real yields (inverse), risk-off flows, central-bank buying, geopolitics.
- Biggest M1 events: US CPI, NFP, FOMC (12:30 / 18:00 UTC-ish), Fed speakers. Spreads spike and stops slip.
- Behaviour: London open expansion, NY open second wave, Asian range often swept at London open.

## Silver, XAGUSD
1 lot = 5,000 oz usually. More volatile than gold in % terms; spreads relatively wider. Correlates with gold.

## Oil (USOIL / WTI, UKOIL / Brent)
EIA inventory Wednesday 10:30 NY, OPEC headlines. Contract often 100 or 1,000 barrels.

## Crypto CFDs (BTCUSD, ETHUSD)
24/7 at some brokers, but spreads can be large relative to M1 ATR and weekend liquidity is thin.
